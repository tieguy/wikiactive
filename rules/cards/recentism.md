# Card: RECENTISM — the news cycle is not history

**Canonical:** [[WP:RECENTISM]] (`rules/canonical/recentism.wikitext`)

## Operative clause

Recent events feel disproportionately important. An article must not let the
last news cycle crowd out the topic's actual weight: recent detail belongs in
proportion, and only what *durable* sources establish survives. Breaking-news
facts (numbers, casualties, attributions) change; wait for the settled
account or attribute explicitly to the day's reporting.

## Load-bearing specifics

- "Currently", "as of now", "recently" — either date the claim
  ("as of 2026") or cut it.
- Single-day coverage of an old topic does not re-weight the article.
- An event is notable enough for the lead only once secondary coverage shows
  it matters to the topic overall.

## Failure examples

- Operator replay fixture: recentism in a frozen article — a burst of
  2020s coverage ballooning the last section while decades of prior history
  sit in two paragraphs.
- "The company currently employs 500 people" (undated, primary PR) → "As of
  [year's] annual report, the company employed 500 people".
