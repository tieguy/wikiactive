# Card: UNDUE — due and undue weight

**Canonical:** [[WP:NPOV#Due and undue weight]] (`rules/canonical/neutral-point-of-view.wikitext`)

## Operative clause

Articles must not give minority views more space than their published support
warrants, nor majority views less. "Due" is measured by the distribution of
*reliable, published* coverage — not by how interesting a view is. Tiny-minority
views (fringe) may be omitted entirely or named only to contextualize the
fringe status itself.

## Load-bearing specifics

- Fringe theories: describe the mainstream view *first and in more detail*;
  the fringe view appears as a fringe view ("Proponents of X claim …, but
  scientific consensus holds …").
- Op-ed/advocacy sources cannot establish weight for a factual claim.
- Article *structure* carries weight too: section count, paragraph length,
  lead placement, See-also ordering.

## Failure examples

- An article devotes two sections to a marginal interpretation covered by one
  primary source, and three sentences to the mainstream account covered by
  dozens of sources → structural UNDUE.
- Game-theory framing of a social topic absent from the literature (operator
  replay fixture: coverage/UNDUE absence as a *judgment* finding — the
  article never covers what RS cover, or covers what they don't).
