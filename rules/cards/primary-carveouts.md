# Card: PRIMARY-CARVEOUTS — when primary sources are allowed

**Canonical:** [[WP:NOR§Primary, secondary and tertiary sources]] (`rules/canonical/no-original-research.wikitext`)

## Operative clause

Primary sources may be used for *descriptive, uncontroversial* facts about
themselves: a company's site for its founding date (attributed or
corroborated), a novel for its plot, a statute for what it says, a dataset
for the numbers it contains. They may NOT support interpretation,
significance, patterns, or anything that requires *you* to connect dots.
Every interpretive claim needs a secondary source.

## Load-bearing specifics

- Plot summaries: the work itself suffices; themes/interpretations need
  criticism.
- Government/statistical data: descriptive numbers fine; "this shows a
  crisis" needs analysis.
- Autobiographical/self-published material about living people: usable only
  for innocuous self-description, never for third-party claims.

## Failure examples

- "The report's data proves the policy failed" — the data shows numbers; the
  failure-judgment needs the analyst who said so.
- Plot + theme paragraph where the theme sentences ride the novel citation.
