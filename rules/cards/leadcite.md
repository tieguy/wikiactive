# Card: LEADCITE — citations in the lead

**Canonical:** [[WP:LEADCITE]] / MOS:LEAD § citations (`rules/canonical/mos-lead.wikitext`, `rules/canonical/citing-sources.wikitext`)

## Operative clause

The lead is a summary of *sourced body content*; body citations usually
suffice. Add a lead citation only when: the claim is contentious, it concerns
a living person, a direct quote appears, the paragraph's topic defies easy
location in the body, or the claim is not restated later. Never pile
citations into the lead as decoration — that is LEADREFBOMB.

## Load-bearing specifics

- L1 rescue's "lead sentence with first citation" is the exception carved by
  the loop: a rescued lead *may* carry one citation where the body is empty.
- Contested/BLP material in the lead: cite inline even if repeated below.
- Unsourced lead claim with no body support is a V problem first (B-cluster),
  LEADCITE second.

## Failure examples

- Lead paragraph ending with five citations covering every clause → move
  detail+refs to body, summarize in lead.
- Contentious characterization in a BLP lead with no citation → cite inline
  or remove.
