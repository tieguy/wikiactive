# Card: NOTLITREVIEW — Wikipedia is not a survey of everything published

**Canonical:** [[WP:NOT§Wikipedia is not a directory... / NOTPAPER]] (`rules/canonical/what-wikipedia-is-not.wikitext`)

## Operative clause

Wikipedia summarizes *what knowledge exists on a topic*, not *everything
published about it*. Loop 4 mines sources *for the article's claims*, one
source at a time; it must not accrete every study, every mention, every
interview. If a source adds no claim the article needs (or only duplicates
already-sourced claims with weaker wording), it is not integrated — it is
logged in the ledger and dropped.

## Load-bearing specifics

- A review article/secondary summary beats five primary studies stacked.
- "More citations" is not a goal: *better-sourced claims* is.
- Surveys of coverage (counts, "many scholars") need a secondary source doing
  the counting — that's what B2 protects.

## Failure examples

- Adding a paragraph per paper because each paper mentions the subject.
- Citing ten sources for one fact because they were all fetched — pick the
  best one or two.
