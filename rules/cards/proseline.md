# Card: PROSELINE — prose, not timeline bullet-itis

**Canonical:** [[WP:MOS§Prose]] / proseline (`rules/canonical/mos.wikitext`)

## Operative clause

Chronology is a narrative, not a ledger. Where a section's paragraphs have
degenerated into "In 2012, X. In 2013, Y. In 2014, Z." — one fact per
sentence, every sentence dated — restructure into thematic prose: group
related events, subordinate minor ones, and let the significant ones carry
the paragraph. Dates serve the story; the story is not the dates.

## Load-bearing specifics

- Lists are for genuinely enumerable content (members, tracks, parameters);
  history is usually prose.
- A date is not a paragraph. If the only thing unifying sentences is that
  they share a year, regroup by *what happened*, then by when.

## Failure examples

- "In 2010 the company released A. In 2011 it released B. In 2012 it
  released C." → "The company released A (2010), B (2011), and C (2012) in
  three consecutive years" or a thematic regrouping — whichever the sources'
  own emphasis supports.
