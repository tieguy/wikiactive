# Card: SUMMARY-SPINOFF — summary style and article splitting

**Canonical:** [[WP:SUMMARYSTYLE]] (`rules/canonical/summary-style.wikitext`)

## Operative clause

When a section outgrows its parent article, the sourced content moves to a
dedicated article (or section) and the parent keeps a 2–3 sentence summary
with a `{{Main}}` hatnote. The summary must stand alone (a reader who stops
there leaves with the right impression) and must not simply cut off mid-thought.
Never leave the parent with a stub-section pointing nowhere, and never keep
the full detail *and* add the split (that's duplication, not summarizing).

## Load-bearing specifics

- Parent summary reflects the child's *due weights*, in miniature.
- `{{Main|Article}}` directly under the section heading.
- The split content keeps its citations — citations do not stay behind.

## Failure examples

- Section ballooned to 8 paragraphs when a good standalone article exists →
  replace with summary + `{{Main}}`.
- Summary that summarizes only the *first* paragraph of the child article.
